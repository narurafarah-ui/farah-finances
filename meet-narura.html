Meet the Accountant

Narura Farah

Founder, Farah Finances

With more than 15 years of accounting experience, Narura has built her practice around one idea: business owners deserve accounting support that is dependable, accurate, and genuinely practical — not generic.

Experience

Hands-on across the full accounting function

Narura has extensive experience managing monthly and year-end close processes, preparing financial statements, and overseeing accounting functions for businesses of all sizes. She's supported companies through ongoing accounting needs as well as focused, project-based work — always bringing the same level of accuracy and attention.

Because every business is different, she doesn't work from a fixed playbook. Each engagement is scoped around what a client actually needs — whether that's full ongoing support or experienced accounting leadership for a specific project.

Philosophy

Clarity, confidence, and control

Narura's approach is straightforward: professionalism, accuracy, and a hands-on presence in every engagement. She believes business owners shouldn't have to guess where their finances stand — so she works to make sure the numbers are always clear, current, and something you can act on with confidence.

"My commitment is simple: reliable accounting that gives you real clarity and control."
